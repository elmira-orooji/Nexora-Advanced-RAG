# Nexora logo system

- Primary: `#32127A`; dark neutral: `#0B0910`; light accent: `#9B86E1`
- Clear space: at least one quarter of the symbol width
- Minimum symbol size: 16 px digital / 5 mm print
- Never add gradients, glow, shadows, outlines, or alter proportions

Use `nexora-horizontal-dark.svg` on light surfaces, `nexora-horizontal-light.svg` on dark surfaces, and `nexora-monochrome.svg` for one-color reproduction.
